Unprojected TopoJSON (WGS84). The site projects it in the browser with
d3-geo's geoAlbersUsa, the same Albers composite projection us-atlas uses
for its preprojected files. Only the 50 states are drawn. The District of
Columbia and the territories are in the file but listed separately in the UI.
